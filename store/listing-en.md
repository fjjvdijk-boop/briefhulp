## Full description (max 4000)

Got a letter? We explain it.

Letters from the Dutch Tax Administration, your municipality, the CJIB, an insurer or a debt collection agency can be hard to understand. With Briefhulp you take a photo of the letter. The app tells you in plain language:

• what the letter says
• what you need to do, step by step
• by when (with a reminder)
• how much to pay, to whom, and with which reference

FREE AND PRIVATE
• The explanation is made on your own phone. Your photo is not sent anywhere.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Works offline.

USEFUL
• Red, orange or green: see at once how urgent a letter is.
• Reminder on your phone, 3 days and 1 day before the deadline.
• Copy the account number and payment reference with one tap. Briefhulp checks the account number.
• Warns you about signs of a scam letter.
• Difficult Dutch words explained.

DISAGREE OR CAN'T PAY?
• Step-by-step plan: objection, appeal, complaint or disputes committee, with the deadline.
• Briefhulp writes a reply letter in Dutch for you: payment plan, more time, objection, tax remission and more.
• Your rights in plain language, and where to get free help, such as Het Juridisch Loket.

FOR EVERYONE
• Built in 13 languages: Dutch, English, Arabic, Turkish, Polish, Ukrainian, Russian, Romanian, Spanish, French, Persian, Somali, Portuguese.
• Read aloud with your phone's voice, also slower.
• Large text, high contrast, works with screen readers.

Briefhulp gives an explanation, not legal or financial advice. Always check important things on the letter itself.
